# Privacy and Consent

This is a short information note. Final legal text should be reviewed by the clinic's legal advisor.

## What Information May Be Stored?

- Clinic and authorized contact information
- Contact details
- Device name, connection details, and technical logs
- Information shared during support requests
- Patient care notes entered by the user

## Why Is It Used?

- To provide the device service
- To perform technical support and issue review
- To run security and record processes
- To respond to user requests

## Sharing

Information should be shared with authorized people only when required for service, support, security, or legal reasons.

## User Responsibility

- Enter personal data only when needed.
- Do not share access details with unauthorized people.
- Follow clinic procedures and applicable privacy rules.

## Consent

Before using fields that include personal data, informing the relevant people and obtaining consent when required is recommended.
